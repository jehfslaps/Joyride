Joyride Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Joyride records the drives you choose to record, replays them with turn-by-turn directions, and suggests fun routes. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

Your recorded drives (the GPS points, times, and the names you give them) are stored only on your iPhone. Joyride has no accounts and no server of its own, and the developer never receives your drives. Deleting the app, or using “Delete all drives” in Settings, removes them.

Location

Joyride uses your location only:

  • while you are recording a drive (after you tap Start, until you tap Stop), including with your phone locked if you allow “Always” access;
  • while you are using directions; and
  • to center the map and find routes near you while the app is open.

You can change location access at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Mapbox

Maps, directions, place search, and the matching of a recorded drive to real roads are provided by Mapbox. To do this, the app sends Mapbox the locations involved (for example, the points of a drive you want to relive, or the start and destination of a route) and Mapbox's navigation software collects location and usage data while directions are running. Mapbox's handling of this data is described in the Mapbox Privacy Policy.

What Joyride does not do

  • No advertising or ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone other than Mapbox, as described above.
  • No analytics collected by the developer.

Children

Joyride is a driving app and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
