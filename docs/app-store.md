## Description
For nights when the drive is the point.

Tap Start, drive wherever the road takes you, and tap Stop. Joyride saves every street and turn, with the date and time. When you want to do that drive again, pick it and get voice turn-by-turn directions along the exact same roads. Miss a turn and it steers you back onto your drive, not toward a shortcut.

Fun routes: enter a destination and choose from scenic, neighborhood, landmark, backroad, and random routes, listed from fastest to longest, so you know what you're getting into.

Surprise me: set a distance, or pick an area like a county or city, and Joyride chooses somewhere random to go. Keep it a secret until you arrive.

Your drives stay on your phone. No accounts, no ads.

Drive safely: set up your route before you go and always follow road signs and traffic laws.

## App Review notes
Joyride records a drive only after the user taps Start and stops when they tap Stop. "Always" location
permission lets recording continue with the screen locked (UIBackgroundModes: location); the app works
without it but asks the user to keep it open. Voice guidance uses background audio while navigating.
To try reliving, first record a short drive (or walk) with Start and Stop; it then appears under Your drives.
